## Brian Kerr

Interaction designer at Google. Two decades in consumer hardware. Independent work at the intersection of agentic AI, physical computing, and interaction design.

I design and prototype AI-native systems where interfaces, agents, and physical hardware meet, drawing on two decades of interaction design at consumer hardware companies including Google, deep physical-computing practice, and shipped agentic AI projects; currently focused on how ambient physical devices earn and communicate trust when driven by autonomous agents.

### Interactive experiments

- **[synth.cards](https://synth.cards)** — Browser-based modular synthesizer. Twelve module types, real patch cables, AudioWorklet audio at 44.1 kHz. [Source](https://github.com/brikerr/Synthesizer)
- **[Every Espresso](https://everyespresso.pro)** — Interactive anatomy of espresso extraction; every shot is a 25-second chemistry experiment. Radial canvas visualization with a 3D puck cross-section and a narrated tour. [Source](https://github.com/brikerr/Espresso)
- **[Fractals & Chaos](https://fractal.courses)** — GPU-accelerated fractal explorer: Mandelbrot, Julia, Burning Ship, Newton. Real-time WebGL shaders with a 3D height map and guided tour. [Source](https://github.com/brikerr/Fractals)
- **[Ocean Depths](https://depth.surf)** — Interactive visualization of the ocean's five pelagic zones. Light, thermoclines, pressure, bioluminescence, and the SOFAR channel with a narrated tour. [Source](https://github.com/brikerr/Ocean)
