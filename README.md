## Brian Kerr

I design the places where interfaces, agents, and physical hardware meet.

Interaction designer with two decades in consumer hardware, now prototyping AI-native systems. My focus: how a device decides when to act on your behalf, and how it earns that permission.

Site: [briankerr.design](https://www.briankerr.design)

### Featured

- **[Ambient Autonomy Eval](https://github.com/brikerr/ambient-autonomy-eval)**: An eval for one ambient-agent decision: should it observe, suggest, act, or refuse? Hand-labeled cases across wearables, car cabins, factories, medical devices, and robots. Run 1 scored 48/50. After defining "safety-related" and adding 4 act cases, Run 3 scored 54/54.
- **[Safety Line](https://safety-line-smoky.vercel.app)**: A RAG reference tool for safety guidance on physical and ambient AI. It answers from 8 public NIST, NHTSA, FDA, OSHA, and AI Safety Report documents, cites page numbers, and says so when the documents don't cover a question.
- **[briankerr.design](https://www.briankerr.design)**: Work, writing, and what I'm doing now.

### Interactive experiments

- **[synth.cards](https://synth.cards)**: Browser modular synthesizer. Twelve module types, real patch cables, AudioWorklet audio at 44.1 kHz. [Source](https://github.com/brikerr/Synthesizer)
- **[Every Espresso](https://everyespresso.pro)**: Interactive anatomy of espresso extraction. Every shot is a 25-second chemistry experiment. [Source](https://github.com/brikerr/Espresso)
- **[Fractals & Chaos](https://fractal.courses)**: GPU-accelerated fractal explorer: Mandelbrot, Julia, Burning Ship, Newton. Real-time WebGL shaders with a 3D height map. [Source](https://github.com/brikerr/Fractals)
- **[Ocean Depths](https://depth.surf)**: The ocean's five pelagic zones. Light, thermoclines, pressure, bioluminescence, and the SOFAR channel. [Source](https://github.com/brikerr/Ocean)
